# Business Analytics – Global Market Performance Analysis

## Overview
This project applies business analytics methods to evaluate market performance across multiple regions (APAC, US, EU, LATAM, Canada, Africa, and EMEA).  
The objective is to analyze profitability, sales, and quantities across categories and sub-categories to uncover key drivers of performance and provide strategic recommendations.

---

## Dataset
The project is based on shipment and sales data from a global shipping company, covering product categories and market performance indicators across regions.  
The dataset includes:  
- Sales, profit, and quantity per category and sub-category  
- Regional performance metrics  
- Market-specific dashboards and trends  

---

## Project Highlights
- **APAC Market Analysis** – Identified profitability leaders and cost management inefficiencies.  
- **US Market Analysis** – Evaluated state-level profitability and product performance, recommending resource allocation strategies.  
- **EU Market Insights** – Highlighted opportunities in bookcases, storage, and technology sectors.  
- **LATAM Analysis** – Suggested cost optimization and improved pricing for underperforming sub-categories.  
- **Canada, Africa, and EMEA** – Evaluated opportunities to increase profitability in technology and office supplies while addressing inefficiencies in high-quantity/low-profit items.
